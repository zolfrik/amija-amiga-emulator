Floppy drive sound samples (.wav) go here.

The easiest way is to let AmiJa fetch them for you: press F12, then
"Optional files" -> "Download...". It downloads the WinUAE drive sounds plugin
from its official site and unpacks the samples into this folder. Nothing is
downloaded without your consent.

To do it by hand, drop the WinUAE samples straight in here, named:

    drive_click_A500.wav
    drive_spin_A500.wav
    drive_startup_A500.wav
    drive_snatch_A500.wav

If this folder is empty the feature simply turns itself off - no samples are
loaded and the drive stays silent, exactly as with floppy.sound.volume=0.
