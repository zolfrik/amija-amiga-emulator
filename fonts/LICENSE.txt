Topaz_a500_v1.0.ttf
===================

The Kickstart 1.x Topaz font as a TrueType file, from the "amigafonts" package
by dMG/t!s^dS! (Trueschool): https://github.com/rewtnull/amigafonts

Released under the GPL with the GNU font exception (GPL-FE):
